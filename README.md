# RFP Response Assistant — live demo

**From RFP pack to a compliant first draft, with every line traced to its source page.**

Live demo: https://nyyoautomation.github.io/rfp-response-assistant/ — no sign-up, fictional data only.

Pick a sector (facilities & cleaning, security, IT services, training, construction), open the fictional RFP pack (RFP / invitation to tender, terms and conditions, technical specification, price schedule) and get:

- requirements extracted with page citations (click any citation to see the highlighted source line)
- a compliance matrix with evidence from the bidder's answer library and gaps flagged
- evaluation criteria and weights
- key dates and the document checklist
- a proposal draft exported to Word (DOCX), plus the matrix as CSV

Everything on this page runs in your browser. The demo uses a rule-based parser on plain-text sample files; the full product adds AI for messy PDFs, scans and tables.

**Human review required** — outputs are drafts. Hosting: isolated workspace, EU or US hosting (data region of your choice), local / on-prem option.
