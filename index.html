      Security & hosting

      Isolated workspace

      Each company gets its own isolated workspace. Your RFPs, prices and past answers are never mixed with anyone else’s.

      EU or US hosting

      Pick the data region of your choice when your workspace is created. Your documents stay in that region.

      Never trained on your data

      Your files are used only to answer your RFPs. Limited, logged access; deletion on request.

      Human review, always

      Every draft is checked by your team before it goes to a buyer. Citations make that check fast.

      Local / on-prem option

      Need documents to stay on your own machines? A local edition with on-device AI is available on request.

      No remote access

      We never take control of your computer and never ask for your passwords. Support is by video call where you share your screen.

      Honest limits

      • AI can be wrong — citations make claims checkable; they don’t make them true. Review is mandatory.
      • Your prices, your decisions — rates come from you. The assistant only adds up the lines you enter.
      • No guaranteed score — the draft follows the published criteria; nobody can predict an evaluation panel.
      • Gaps stay gaps — when your library has no evidence, the assistant flags it instead of inventing an answer.
      • This demo — the fictional files are plain text. Extraction here uses a rule-based parser running in your browser; the full product adds AI for messy PDFs, scans and tables.

      Questions

      Which file types will the product read?

      PDF (including scanned pages), Word and Excel. Price schedules in Excel keep their line structure.

      Does it work for public-sector and private RFPs?

      Yes — RFPs, RFQs, ITTs and tenders from public buyers or companies. The sector selector above shows five examples.

      Where is my data stored?

      In an isolated workspace, in the data region you choose (EU or US). A local / on-prem edition keeps everything on your own machines.

      Can I try it on my own RFP?

      The public demo uses fictional files only. Trials on your own documents are not open yet; ask on LaborX.

      Do you submit bids for us?

      No. You review, decide and submit. The assistant prepares the draft and the checklists.
