TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
Westmark City Council (fictional) · WCC-CW-2026-19

1. Purpose
This specification sets out the minimum requirements for: community sports hall refurbishment — design and build. 'Shall' means a mandatory requirement; 'should' means a desirable requirement that will be considered in scoring.

2. Requirements (part 1)
2.1 Programme. The hall shall be closed to the public for no more than 14 consecutive weeks, and the changing rooms shall be usable by week 10.
2.2 Roof. The roof build-up shall achieve a thermal transmittance (U-value) of 0.18 W/m2K or better.
2.3 Ventilation. The new ventilation system shall include heat recovery with a seasonal efficiency of at least 75%.

--- Page 2 ---
3. Requirements (part 2)
3.1 Floor. The sports floor shall meet the performance class for multi-sport use set out in Annex D and be tested after installation.
3.2 Accessibility. Changing rooms shall include at least two accessible cubicles and one adult changing facility.
3.3 Quality. The contractor shall operate inspection and test plans for each work package and share them weekly.

--- Page 3 ---
4. Requirements (part 3)
4.1 Waste. At least 70% of construction waste by weight should be diverted from landfill, with monthly reporting.
4.2 Neighbours. Noisy works shall be limited to 08:00–18:00 on weekdays and 08:00–13:00 on Saturdays.
4.3 Information. The contractor should provide a building information model (BIM) of the completed works at handover.
