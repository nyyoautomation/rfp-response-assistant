REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — Community sports hall refurbishment — design and build
Westmark City Council (fictional) · WCC-CW-2026-19

1. Introduction
Westmark City Council (fictional) invites proposals for: community sports hall refurbishment — design and build.
Scope: Refurbishment of a 2,400 m2 (25,800 sq ft) community sports hall: roof insulation and membrane, new ventilation with heat recovery, sports floor replacement and accessible changing rooms, with the hall closed for no more than 14 weeks.

2. Timetable
Clarification questions close: 2026-11-17 at 17:00
Mandatory site visit: 2026-11-20 at 09:30
Proposal submission deadline: 2026-12-01 at 12:00
Proposal validity: 120 days after the submission deadline
Planned start on site: 2027-04-06
All times are local time at the buyer's offices. Late proposals will not be considered.

3. Evaluation criteria
Proposals will be scored against the criteria and weightings below. Quality questions are scored from 0 to 5 and then weighted.
Q1 — Technical approach and methodology — 30% — Method statements for roof, ventilation, floor and changing rooms, and how quality is controlled.
Q2 — Programme and phasing — 15% — Realistic programme within the 14-week closure, with phasing and float.
Q3 — Project team and experience — 15% — Named project manager, site team and similar projects.
Q4 — Health, safety and environment — 10% — Site safety, waste management and disruption to neighbours.
P1 — Price — 30% — Total evaluated price from the priced bill of quantities.

4. Documents to be returned
Bidders shall return the following documents. A proposal missing any mandatory document may be rejected as non-compliant.
- Signed form of tender (Mandatory)
- Technical proposal answering Q1 to Q4 (max 25 pages) (Mandatory)
- Priced bill of quantities (Mandatory)
- Outline construction programme (Mandatory)
- Proof of insurance cover (Mandatory)
- Health and safety policy (Mandatory)
- Three similar project references (Mandatory)
- Priced variant for heat-recovery upgrade (Optional)

5. Instructions to bidders
Proposals shall be submitted in English through the buyer's e-procurement portal. Email submissions will not be accepted.
Questions shall be raised through the portal before the clarification deadline. Answers will be shared with all bidders without naming the bidder who asked.
Bidders shall not contact evaluators directly during the procurement. Canvassing will lead to exclusion.
The buyer is not bound to accept the lowest priced or any proposal and will not reimburse bid costs.
