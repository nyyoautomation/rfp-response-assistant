REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — Leadership and safety training programme for operational staff
Port of Easthaven Authority (fictional) · PEA-LD-2026-02

1. Introduction
Port of Easthaven Authority (fictional) invites proposals for: leadership and safety training programme for operational staff.
Scope: Design and delivery of a blended leadership programme for 120 supervisors and a refreshed safety-awareness course for 900 operational staff, with an online learning platform and impact reporting over 2 years.

2. Timetable
Clarification questions close: 2026-11-23 at 17:00
Proposal submission deadline: 2026-12-07 at 12:00
Shortlisted bidder presentations: 2026-12-15
Proposal validity: 90 days after the submission deadline
Planned programme launch: 2027-03-01
All times are local time at the buyer's offices. Late proposals will not be considered.

3. Evaluation criteria
Proposals will be scored against the criteria and weightings below. Quality questions are scored from 0 to 5 and then weighted.
Q1 — Programme design and learning approach — 30% — Structure, learning methods, alignment with the Authority's competency framework.
Q2 — Trainers and delivery capacity — 20% — Trainer qualifications, sector experience and capacity across shifts.
Q3 — Learning platform and accessibility — 15% — Online platform, accessibility, offline access and reporting.
Q4 — Evaluation and impact — 10% — How learning transfer and business impact are measured and reported.
P1 — Price — 25% — Total evaluated price from the price schedule.

4. Documents to be returned
Bidders shall return the following documents. A proposal missing any mandatory document may be rejected as non-compliant.
- Signed form of tender (Mandatory)
- Technical proposal answering Q1 to Q4 (max 20 pages) (Mandatory)
- Completed price schedule (Mandatory)
- Trainer CVs for the proposed team (Mandatory)
- Sample module outline and materials (Mandatory)
- Accessibility statement for the platform (Mandatory)
- Three client references (Mandatory)
- Sample evaluation report (Optional)

5. Instructions to bidders
Proposals shall be submitted in English through the buyer's e-procurement portal. Email submissions will not be accepted.
Questions shall be raised through the portal before the clarification deadline. Answers will be shared with all bidders without naming the bidder who asked.
Bidders shall not contact evaluators directly during the procurement. Canvassing will lead to exclusion.
The buyer is not bound to accept the lowest priced or any proposal and will not reimburse bid costs.
