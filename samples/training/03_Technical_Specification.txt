TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
Port of Easthaven Authority (fictional) · PEA-LD-2026-02

1. Purpose
This specification sets out the minimum requirements for: leadership and safety training programme for operational staff. 'Shall' means a mandatory requirement; 'should' means a desirable requirement that will be considered in scoring.

2. Requirements (part 1)
2.1 Design. The leadership programme shall be mapped to the Authority's supervisor competency framework in Annex B, with learning outcomes for each module.
2.2 Delivery. Face-to-face sessions shall be delivered on site in groups of no more than 16 participants, scheduled around 24/7 shift patterns.
2.3 Delivery. The safety-awareness course shall be available as a 45-minute e-learning module and as a classroom session for staff without regular computer access.

--- Page 2 ---
3. Requirements (part 2)
3.1 Trainers. Lead trainers shall hold a recognised training qualification and have at least 5 years' experience in operational or industrial settings.
3.2 Platform. The learning platform should meet recognised web accessibility guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA) and work on mobile devices.
3.3 Platform. Completion records shall be exportable to the Authority's HR system in CSV format at least monthly.

--- Page 3 ---
4. Requirements (part 3)
4.1 Evaluation. The supplier shall evaluate the programme at reaction, learning and behaviour levels and report results quarterly.
4.2 Languages. Safety materials should be available in plain English and in two additional languages to be agreed.
4.3 Account management. The supplier shall provide a named account manager who attends a monthly review meeting on site.
