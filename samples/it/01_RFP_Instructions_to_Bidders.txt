REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — Managed IT service desk and end-user support
Riverton Regional Health Network (fictional) · RRHN-IT-2026-04

1. Introduction
Riverton Regional Health Network (fictional) invites proposals for: managed it service desk and end-user support.
Scope: 24/7 service desk, on-site end-user support at 6 clinics, device lifecycle management for about 2,400 devices and monthly service reporting, for a 3-year term with an optional 2-year extension.

2. Timetable
Clarification questions close: 2026-11-16 at 17:00
Supplier Q&A session (online): 2026-11-19 at 15:00
Proposal submission deadline: 2026-11-30 at 12:00
Shortlisted bidder demonstrations: 2026-12-09
Proposal validity: 90 days after the submission deadline
All times are local time at the buyer's offices. Late proposals will not be considered.

3. Evaluation criteria
Proposals will be scored against the criteria and weightings below. Quality questions are scored from 0 to 5 and then weighted.
Q1 — Service desk model and service levels — 30% — How the desk is staffed, how tickets flow and how service levels are met and reported.
Q2 — Security and data protection — 20% — Access control, patient data protection, incident response and certifications.
Q3 — On-site support and device lifecycle — 15% — Clinic support model, device imaging, repair and secure disposal.
Q4 — Transition and continuous improvement — 10% — Knowledge transfer from the incumbent and how the service improves over time.
P1 — Price — 25% — Total evaluated price from the price schedule.

4. Documents to be returned
Bidders shall return the following documents. A proposal missing any mandatory document may be rejected as non-compliant.
- Signed form of tender (Mandatory)
- Technical proposal answering Q1 to Q4 (max 30 pages) (Mandatory)
- Completed price schedule (Mandatory)
- Information security certificate or audit report (Mandatory)
- Completed security questionnaire (Annex C) (Mandatory)
- Draft service level agreement (Mandatory)
- Three client references (Mandatory)
- Sample monthly service report (Optional)

5. Instructions to bidders
Proposals shall be submitted in English through the buyer's e-procurement portal. Email submissions will not be accepted.
Questions shall be raised through the portal before the clarification deadline. Answers will be shared with all bidders without naming the bidder who asked.
Bidders shall not contact evaluators directly during the procurement. Canvassing will lead to exclusion.
The buyer is not bound to accept the lowest priced or any proposal and will not reimburse bid costs.
