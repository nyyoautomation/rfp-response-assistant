TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
Lakeside Metropolitan University (fictional) · LMU-FM-2026-07

1. Purpose
This specification sets out the minimum requirements for: campus cleaning and janitorial services — 3-year contract. 'Shall' means a mandatory requirement; 'should' means a desirable requirement that will be considered in scoring.

2. Requirements (part 1)
2.1 Scope. The contractor shall clean all teaching rooms, offices and washrooms daily between 06:00 and 09:00, before the start of classes.
2.2 Quality. The contractor shall carry out monthly joint quality audits with the University and reach an average audit score of at least 85 out of 100.

--- Page 2 ---
3. Requirements (part 2)
3.1 Response time. Reactive cleaning requests (spills, washroom failures) shall be attended within 30 minutes during staffed hours.
3.2 Staffing. Each building shall have a named supervisor on site during every shift, with a supervision ratio of no more than one supervisor per 12 cleaning staff.
3.3 Training. All staff shall complete chemical handling and manual handling training before their first shift, with records available on request.

4. Requirements (part 3)
4.1 Sustainability. Cleaning chemicals shall carry a recognised ecolabel and microfibre systems shall be used to reduce water and chemical use.
4.2 Waste. The contractor shall segregate general waste, mixed recycling and food waste at source and report monthly tonnages.
4.3 Technology. The bidder should describe any digital tools used to record cleaning tasks, audits and requests in real time.
