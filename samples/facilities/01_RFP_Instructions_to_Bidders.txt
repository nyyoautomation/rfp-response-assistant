REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — Campus cleaning and janitorial services — 3-year contract
Lakeside Metropolitan University (fictional) · LMU-FM-2026-07

1. Introduction
Lakeside Metropolitan University (fictional) invites proposals for: campus cleaning and janitorial services — 3-year contract.
Scope: Daily cleaning, periodic deep cleaning, washroom hygiene and waste segregation across 14 campus buildings (approx. 61,000 m2 / 656,000 sq ft), with an option for two 12-month extensions.

2. Timetable
Clarification questions close: 2026-11-20 at 17:00
Optional site walk-through: 2026-11-24 at 10:00
Proposal submission deadline: 2026-12-04 at 12:00
Proposal validity: 120 days after the submission deadline
Planned contract start: 2027-02-01
All times are local time at the buyer's offices. Late proposals will not be considered.

3. Evaluation criteria
Proposals will be scored against the criteria and weightings below. Quality questions are scored from 0 to 5 and then weighted.
Q1 — Service delivery and quality plan — 30% — How the bidder will deliver the specification, inspect quality and fix defects.
Q2 — Staffing, training and supervision — 20% — Staffing model per building, supervision ratio, training and vetting.
Q3 — Sustainability and safe chemicals — 15% — Certified low-impact products, waste segregation and reduction of water use.
Q4 — Mobilisation and continuity — 10% — Transition plan from the incumbent and cover for absence or peaks.
P1 — Price — 25% — Total evaluated price from the price schedule, scored relative to the lowest compliant bid.

4. Documents to be returned
Bidders shall return the following documents. A proposal missing any mandatory document may be rejected as non-compliant.
- Signed form of tender (Mandatory)
- Technical proposal answering Q1 to Q4 (max 20 pages) (Mandatory)
- Completed price schedule (Mandatory)
- Proof of insurance cover (Mandatory)
- Three client references from the last 5 years (Mandatory)
- Health and safety policy and method statements (Mandatory)
- Product data sheets for cleaning chemicals (Mandatory)
- Sample staff rota for one building (Optional)

5. Instructions to bidders
Proposals shall be submitted in English through the buyer's e-procurement portal. Email submissions will not be accepted.
Questions shall be raised through the portal before the clarification deadline. Answers will be shared with all bidders without naming the bidder who asked.
Bidders shall not contact evaluators directly during the procurement. Canvassing will lead to exclusion.
The buyer is not bound to accept the lowest priced or any proposal and will not reimburse bid costs.
