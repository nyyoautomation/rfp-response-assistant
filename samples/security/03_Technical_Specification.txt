TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
Harbor County Transit Authority (fictional) · HCTA-SEC-2026-11

1. Purpose
This specification sets out the minimum requirements for: manned guarding and mobile patrol services for stations and depots. 'Shall' means a mandatory requirement; 'should' means a desirable requirement that will be considered in scoring.

2. Requirements (part 1)
2.1 Coverage. The contractor shall provide 24-hour, 7-day manned guarding at each depot listed in Annex A, with no post left unattended during shift changes.
2.2 Patrols. Mobile patrol vehicles shall visit every station at least four times per night between 22:00 and 06:00, with each visit recorded electronically.
2.3 Licensing. Every officer shall hold a valid security licence for the role before deployment and carry it while on duty.

--- Page 2 ---
3. Requirements (part 2)
3.1 Training. Officers shall complete conflict de-escalation and first aid training before deployment and refresh it at least every 24 months.
3.2 Incidents. Serious incidents shall be reported by phone to the Authority's control room within 15 minutes and in writing within 24 hours.
3.3 Supervision. A shift supervisor shall be on duty at all times and shall make at least one welfare check per officer per shift.

4. Requirements (part 3)
4.1 Technology. The bidder should describe the guard-tour and reporting system used and how the Authority can access live data.
4.2 Contingency. The contractor shall be able to supply up to 10 additional officers within 12 hours for special events or emergencies.
4.3 Station procedures. Officers deployed at stations shall be trained in the Authority's evacuation procedures within their first week on site.
