REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — Manned guarding and mobile patrol services for stations and depots
Harbor County Transit Authority (fictional) · HCTA-SEC-2026-11

1. Introduction
Harbor County Transit Authority (fictional) invites proposals for: manned guarding and mobile patrol services for stations and depots.
Scope: 24/7 manned guarding at 3 depots and 9 stations, mobile patrols between sites, CCTV monitoring support and incident reporting, for an initial term of 3 years.

2. Timetable
Clarification questions close: 2026-11-18 at 16:00
Bidder briefing (online): 2026-11-21 at 14:00
Proposal submission deadline: 2026-12-02 at 12:00
Proposal validity: 90 days after the submission deadline
Planned contract start: 2027-01-15
All times are local time at the buyer's offices. Late proposals will not be considered.

3. Evaluation criteria
Proposals will be scored against the criteria and weightings below. Quality questions are scored from 0 to 5 and then weighted.
Q1 — Operational approach and post orders — 30% — How posts, patrols and CCTV support will be run and supervised around the clock.
Q2 — Officer licensing, vetting and training — 20% — Licensing status, vetting, initial and refresher training, including de-escalation.
Q3 — Incident management and reporting — 15% — Incident response, escalation, reporting times and use of technology.
Q4 — Transition and contingency — 10% — Mobilisation from the incumbent and cover for absence or major events.
P1 — Price — 25% — Total evaluated price from the price schedule.

4. Documents to be returned
Bidders shall return the following documents. A proposal missing any mandatory document may be rejected as non-compliant.
- Signed form of tender (Mandatory)
- Technical proposal answering Q1 to Q4 (max 25 pages) (Mandatory)
- Completed price schedule (Mandatory)
- Security business licence (Mandatory)
- Proof of insurance cover (Mandatory)
- Three client references (Mandatory)
- Sample patrol report (Optional)
- Organisation chart for the contract (Mandatory)

5. Instructions to bidders
Proposals shall be submitted in English through the buyer's e-procurement portal. Email submissions will not be accepted.
Questions shall be raised through the portal before the clarification deadline. Answers will be shared with all bidders without naming the bidder who asked.
Bidders shall not contact evaluators directly during the procurement. Canvassing will lead to exclusion.
The buyer is not bound to accept the lowest priced or any proposal and will not reimburse bid costs.
